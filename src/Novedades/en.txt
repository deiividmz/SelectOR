Scheduled services|The owner or a manager schedules shifts with a route, itinerary, train and departure window. Members reserve them and, with «Prepare», the itinerary is loaded into the road book. When it is done, the company gets a bonus and the driver adds completed shifts to their profile.
SelectOR servers|Multiplayer has official rooms: join with one click, with the route, server and password already set. Each room shows how many players it has and who can join.
Users online|The home screen shows live how many users are online and how many are driving, with links to the SelectOR website and Discord.
Train type in Fleet|With «Type and seats…» the company decides whether a train carries passengers or freight, and its seats.
New windows|Notices, forms and dialogs with a more polished design, with their icon, and a dark title bar.
Stops without passengers|Fixed the stops where sometimes nobody got on or off.
Authorised routes|A version of a route that is almost the same as one already authorised is authorised automatically.
