1.2.92
Driver and dispatcher certificates|In My profile → Certificates you take the exam and level up (B, A and A+) with your services. To take command as a dispatcher you need to pass its exam. Certified players carry their badge in the room, the chat and the radio, and companies can ask for a certificate in their Board job offers.
Tips on the Board|A new section with tips to earn more on every trip: fares, passengers by time of day and weather, and more, with current figures.
Faster routes|In the control centre, click the signal and click the destination: the route is drawn straight away. Right-click for more options, and «Replay» sets the last ones again.
Only your signal boxes|The «Only mine» button in the control centre shows only the signal boxes you run, and it's remembered.
Disconnect from the control centre|Dispatchers can disconnect a player or remove loose wagons within their signal boxes, always with a reason that the player sees.
Applicant profile|Managers can see the profile of whoever asks to join their company before accepting them.
Every passenger counts in the league|The league and your service log count every passenger you carry, even though fares have their limit.
More compatible controllers|Settings → Controllers: throttle + train brake lever, a combined lever that adapts to each train (with its rest point, automatic or manual) and buttons that stay on until the next press.
Snappier set speed|In Stable, Testing and CYZYRails the cruise control set speed moves as quickly as in New Year.
Lights on Breclav - Praha|With CYZYRails, the stations on the Breclav - Praha route light up in yellow.
Steadier room trains|Your Open Rails no longer stalls with other players' trains that leave their path, and an AI train no longer couples to yours.
No Ctrl+9 in the room|In the shared room the Ctrl+9 window no longer opens with any Open Rails version.
