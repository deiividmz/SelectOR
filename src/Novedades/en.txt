1.2.79
AI train doors|In the shared room, AI trains open their doors on the platform side when they stop at each station and close them before leaving.
AI trains up to the buffer stop|At terminus stations, such as Estación Sur or Aeropuerto, AI trains now pull up to about 15 m from the end of the track instead of stopping in the middle of the platform.
No more AI jams at stations|If an AI train's track at a station is occupied, it takes another free track of the same station and makes its stop there, instead of waiting at the entrance.
Commercial stops only for passenger trains|The service sheet no longer shows commercial stops or door opening for freight trains or trains without passengers.
