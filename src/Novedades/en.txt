Control centre on a single screen|The whole route on one board you pan with the mouse and zoom with the wheel, following the real track layout (spread out in stations so it reads well): occupied blocks, routes, numbered switches, signals with their real look, platforms and trains. The list on the left takes you to each station or line section, and the dispatcher works from the same board.
Room switches as on the map|In the shared room your Open Rails no longer sets switches by itself: the switches you see in 3D are always the same as on the big map and in the room, also when another player throws them.
G key in the room|In the shared room, G and Shift+G throw the switch in the room, like a click on the big map (before, it only changed in your Open Rails and the others did not see it).
Detached cars on the map|If someone uncouples and leaves cars on the track, they show on the big map and in the control centre («… detached cars», in grey) and occupy their track: signals and routes take them into account.
Lit tunnels in CYZYRails|Tunnel lamps no longer go dark when you switch on the train lights.
Finer ATO in CYZYRails|The ATO starts braking in time and follows its braking curve, without overshooting the stopping point.
