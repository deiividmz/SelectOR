Open Rails no longer closes|If the connection to the room drops, Open Rails stays open instead of closing suddenly, with any Open Rails version.
Typing in the chat without touching the train|While you type in the HUD chat, Open Rails doesn't receive the keys.
Switches from the big map|Switches are requested just like with Ctrl+9: if the dispatcher refuses one, the map tells you why.
Trains visible from afar|On the maps, trains don't become tiny when you zoom out and they show their name.
Who is in the room|On the Route page of CGL_V22N_RE you see how many players are in the shared room and who they are.
Shifts on certain days|Scheduled services can repeat on certain weekdays (for example, Monday to Friday) and, without a specific train, show a passenger or freight train.
Review always with a map|Each infraction's map and diagram show even if you don't have the route installed.
HUD as before|The HUD is back to its usual size.
