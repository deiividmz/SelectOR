Your train's signals in the room|Ask the signal ahead to clear with Tab and release your signal with Ctrl+Tab, also from the big map. Other signal orders are for the administrator.
Back where you should be|If you come back to the room with another path or train, you start where the new one says; with the same one, you get your train back.
Spain's sun on CGL|On the CGL routes the sun rises and sets at Spain's real time: at 19:00 in October it is still daylight.
CGL timetables as PDF|On the Route page of CGL_V22N_RE you have GiorgioTrains' Regional and Cercanías timetables to open or save.
HUD at your scale|On screens with Windows scaling above 100%, the HUD, the big map and the road book show at that size.
